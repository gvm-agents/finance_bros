# Today's plan

**Goal:** by the end of the day, measure the benefit of hosted open models on our tasks, against a frozen baseline.

**Done means:** one number improved from the baseline, with its cost, what it did not fix, and a working demo.

## Tracks

Pick one. You can bring your own task, as long as it uses public or synthetic data.

| Track | You do | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Forms | Tune extraction: models, thinking, cost | Anyone new to benchmarks |
| Deep research | Data center funding and algorithm effects | Analysts, finance |
| News | Feeds, story grouping, dashboard | Front-end, data engineers |
| Router and training | Route across models; train a small model | ML engineers |
| Your own | Your task on this bench | Anyone with a real problem |

## Working together

- **Claim:** say your track in the group chat.
- **Branch:** `git switch -c your-track`. `main` must always run.
- **Save:** commit and push every 30 minutes.
- **Hand off:** add a line to `HANDOFF.md`: what works, what does not, what is next.
- **Update:** one line to the group chat at each stop time: the number, and what you need.
