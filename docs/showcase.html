That compares with a mid-priced frontier model. A small frontier model costs about the same as our router, so the savings depend on which frontier model you would otherwise use.

Three ways to maximize the benefits of open models

Route across models

Send each item to the cheapest model that can handle it. A low-cost open model reads everything. A second, free model double-checks whatever the first calls routine.

Result: frontier-level catch rate at about 1/16 of the cost. About half of the items needed the second look.

Build a smarter harness

Surround the models with the controls a business needs: a hard spending limit, automatic checks on every answer, and no ability for a model to act on its own.

Result: if a malicious document fools a model, the worst case is one wrong answer that a check or a person can catch.

Train a specialized model

Teach a small open model one narrow job, using examples where the right answer is already known.

Result: almost no false alarms and much shorter answers, but it missed slightly more. Early and still being tested.

On this test the laptop model alone matched our router, at no fee. So for volumes one laptop can handle, about 2,600 items an hour, the laptop alone is the better choice. The router earns its small cost when volume is higher, about 5,000 items an hour by our estimate, or when the work cannot depend on one machine staying on. The laptop model is also much weaker on writing and formatting, as the next chart shows.

No model wins every job

Choose the job first, then the model. Pulling facts from forms is close to solved. Exact formatting is hard for every model.

We also audited our own test. We found and fixed four faults, re-marked all saved answers, and retested on new documents after settings were locked.

What the platform does

  • Recommends a setup for a task: which models, how many running at once, budget and time.
  • Applies business rules. Changing a rule, such as which models are allowed, changes the setup, even during a run.
  • Runs and checks the work with a hard spending limit, and sends exceptions to a person.
  • Shows results on dashboards, from the big picture down to the source document.
